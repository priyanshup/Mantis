1. The whole site on one page

Six tabs sit inside one page. Cards, people and the library are separate pages you reach from them. Red boxes are pages with no way in (or almost none).

Green boxes in the diagrams below lead to another page. Blue boxes are things Mantis stores. Dashed arrows are weak or missing links.

2. Today

The job: what do I do now, and what changed since the last pull.

3. Meetings

The job: prepare for a meeting and keep the weekly list right.

Removing a meeting keeps notes already in the inbox.

4. Features, the feature view and the cards

The job: see what you own and where each thing stands. A feature opens inside the app first; the full card is a second page.

Inside the feature view: Needs you links back to Today (tasks) and Questions (open questions); Back returns to Features. Both the feature view and the full card hold a Confluence page, copy buttons and the Jira link, but the tabs differ.

5. Questions

The job: settle what is open. Nothing changes the moment you answer; the answer waits in the inbox.

6. Sources

The job: feed Mantis and see what it read. Only you start a pull.

The Inbox icon in the header lands here. It counts answers, page edits and files waiting for the next pull.

7. Growth

The job: keep your record of wins and what to step up on. Mantis proposes; only you add.

8. Tools in the header

Present on every page, so they are the easiest way to get around.

9. Library and written pages

Pages, epics and stories ready to copy into Confluence and Jira, plus diagrams and decisions.
