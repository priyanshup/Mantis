# WRITING_STYLE

Source: the humanizer skill (https://github.com/blader/humanizer), based on Wikipedia's
"Signs of AI writing". It is installed in this environment as `/humanizer`.

## When to use it

Run it on any document, output, summary or message Mantis produces for a reader. Skip it
for registers, digests and other structured records where wording must stay verbatim to
the source.

## What it removes (26 patterns, 6 groups)

- **Staging instead of stating:** "not X but Y", one-line closers, profound-sounding sayings, run-ups before the point, arguing against objections nobody raised.
- **Rhythm by rule:** forced triads, repeated sentence openings, dashes everywhere, stacked qualifiers, hyphenated pairs, needless passive voice.
- **Inflation and borrowed authority:** stock AI words, inflated significance, vague links, shallow "-ing" riders, sales language, appeals to unnamed experts, avoiding plain verbs like "is".
- **Formatting by rule:** decorative bold, ornamental headings, curly quotes.
- **Draft leftovers:** chatbot residue, knowledge disclaimers, repeated headings, meta-commentary.
- **Wrong reader:** re-explaining what the reader already knows.

## How to apply

1. Draft with the facts and citations first. Meaning and sources never change in the rewrite.
2. Run the rewrite, then check that every cited claim still carries its citation.
3. For a personal voice, give it two or three writing samples from the owner and add them below.

## Voice samples
