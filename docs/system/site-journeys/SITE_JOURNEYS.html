Mantis at a glance

Mantis is the product owner's personal workspace. It reads Jira, Confluence and the owner's notes, keeps everything in one place, and shows what needs attention today.

3. What each screen is for

Read each card as: why you come here, what you can do, where it takes you.

Today

What do I do now?

You can
Tick off, postpone or add tasks. See who owes you something and copy a reminder message.
Leads to
The feature behind a change. Sources, when you start an update.

Meetings

Am I ready for this week?

You can
Browse the week by day. Open a meeting to see people and topics to raise. Ask Mantis for a briefing. Add or change meetings.
Leads to
The people in the meeting.

Features

Where does each thing stand?

You can
Follow or archive features. Open one to see its stage, epic, stories, Confluence page and what needs you.
Leads to
The feature page, or a topic page.

Questions

What is still undecided?

You can
Filter by person or feature. Read the background. Type an answer.
What happens
The answer is saved and Mantis uses it at the next update. Nothing changes immediately.

Sources

What has Mantis read?

You can
Add a file or note. Choose what to fetch from Jira and Confluence. Start an update. See what needs attention.
What happens
Only the owner starts an update. It runs in the background.

Growth

What should I step up on?

You can
Review suggestions from Mantis, add your own wins and learning, see where the evidence is thin.
What happens
Mantis proposes. Only the owner adds to the record.

4. What we found

Checked by following every link on the built site. Each item is a decision for the owner: connect it, or remove it.

No way in

What's new page

A page listing the last 30 days of changes. No button or link anywhere leads to it.

Add a link from Today, or delete the page.

Hard to find

Epics and stories board

One board showing every epic and story by status. Only one rare kind of page links to it.

Link it from Features, or delete it.

Two of the same

Two pages per feature

Each feature has an in-app view and a separate full page. They overlap but show different tabs.

Decide whether both are needed.

Also: four topic and feature pages could not be reached from static links (they may appear in the Features list, which needs one click to confirm); the feature view is still called "promotions" in the code; and 16 old addresses only redirect and can be deleted.
